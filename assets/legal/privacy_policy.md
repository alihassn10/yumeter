# Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains what Yumeter collects, why, who else handles it, and how you can see, change or delete it. Yumeter is run by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we"). For any question or request, email [CONTACT EMAIL].

## What we collect

**Your account.** Your email address, used to sign you in with a one-time code. We never ask for a password.

**Your profile.** The answers you give when setting up your plan: sex, birth date, height, weight, activity level, goal, target weight, weekly pace, how you like to eat, and whether you use metric or imperial units. Some of this is information about your health.

**What you log.** Each meal's name, type, time, calories and macros, and how it was logged (photo, barcode, search or by hand). Photos you take or choose to estimate a meal. Your weigh-ins, one per day.

**Usage counts.** How many meal photos you have analysed today, so we can apply the daily limit.

**Technical data.** Our hosting provider keeps short-lived server logs, which include IP addresses, request times and error details, to keep the service running and secure.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell or rent your data.

## Why we use it

- To run your account and keep your log in sync across your devices.
- To work out your daily calorie and macro targets from your profile.
- To estimate the nutrition of meals from your photos.
- To look up packaged food by name or barcode.
- To prevent abuse, for example through the daily limit on photo analyses.

We use your data because you asked us to provide the service. Where we process health information, we do so because you chose to enter it to get a personalised plan. You can change or remove it at any time.

## Who else handles your data

We use a small number of providers to run Yumeter. Each handles only what it needs:

- **Supabase** hosts our database, sign-in, photo storage and server functions, in [REGION]. Everything listed under "What we collect" is stored there.
- **Google (Gemini API)** receives a meal photo when you ask for an estimate, and returns the estimate. We ask Google not to store the photo for later retrieval. - **Our email provider** ([EMAIL PROVIDER]) sends your sign-in codes and receives your email address to do so.
- **Open Food Facts**, a public food database, receives what you type into food search and the barcodes you scan. These requests come from your device and are not linked to your Yumeter account.

Some of these providers may process data outside your country. Where the law requires it, they do so under appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. A meal's photo is deleted when you delete the meal. Photos that were uploaded but never saved with a meal are deleted automatically within about a week.

When you delete your account, we delete your profile, meals, weigh-ins, photos and usage counts straight away. Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a short period until those backups expire, and server logs expire on their own schedule.

## Your choices and rights

- **See and correct** your profile, meals and weigh-ins in the app at any time.
- **Delete** any meal or weigh-in in the app, or your whole account from Settings, Delete account.
- **Get a copy** of your data by emailing [CONTACT EMAIL].
- **Object or ask questions** about how we use your data by emailing us.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

## Children

Yumeter is not meant for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

## Security

Your data travels over encrypted connections. Each account can reach only its own records, and photos are kept in private storage that is only shown through short-lived links. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you about a breach that affects you where the law requires.

## Not medical advice

Yumeter gives estimates from a standard formula and from photos. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.
